# About Telink Semiconductor

**Telink Semiconductor** is a leading fabless semiconductor company dedicated to the
design and development of low-power, high-performance wireless connectivity System-on-Chip
(SoC) solutions. Founded in 2010 and headquartered in Shanghai, China, Telink has grown
to become a global player in the IoT connectivity space.

---

## Our Mission

To provide best-in-class wireless connectivity solutions that power the next generation
of IoT devices — enabling smarter, more connected, and more energy-efficient products
across consumer, industrial, and automotive markets.

---

## Technology Leadership

Telink's SoCs integrate a rich set of wireless protocols, offering true multi-protocol
support on a single chip:

| Protocol | Certification | Key Applications |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| Bluetooth LE | Bluetooth SIG Board Member | IoT, wearables, beacons |
| Bluetooth Mesh | Bluetooth SIG | Smart lighting, building automation |
| Zigbee | CSA Member | Smart home, industrial control |
| Thread | Thread Group Contributor | Matter over Thread |
| Matter | CSA Member | Smart home interoperability |
| Wi-Fi | - | High-bandwidth IoT |
| 2.4GHz Proprietary | - | Gaming, HID devices |
